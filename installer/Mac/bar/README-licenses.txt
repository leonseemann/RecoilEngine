Recoil engine for BAR on macOS

This archive contains the Recoil engine (GPL v2 or later, see LICENSE and
gpl-2.0.txt), its pr-downloader and the libraries listed in this folder, each
under its own license (Mesa, LLVM, SDL, OpenAL Soft, ...). The BAR launcher
for macOS downloads it: https://github.com/leonseemann/spring-launcher

Source code of the engine build: https://github.com/leonseemann/RecoilEngine

The Beyond All Reason game, the BYAR-Chobby lobby and all game artwork are NOT
part of this archive. The launcher downloads them from the official Beyond All
Reason servers and they are subject to their own licenses, see
https://github.com/beyond-all-reason/Beyond-All-Reason/blob/master/LICENSE.md
